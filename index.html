The cabin

Built by hand, hung with dreamcatchers

Shangrila Rénao sits on Tandi hill, away from the road and its noise. The walls and roof are mostly glass, so the valley is in the room all day and the sky is in it all night.

Pine panelling runs across the ceiling and up the walls, a slate feature wall holds the paintings, and an emerald sofa faces the window. You get the whole cabin and the grounds around it.

Questions

Before you book

How far is the walk from parking?

About 70 metres on a footpath. Bring backpacks instead of trolley bags and the hosts will help with the rest.

Is it suitable for elderly guests?

The path can be a little hard for older guests. Plan to arrive in daylight and tell the hosts if you need extra help.

Is parking free?

Yes. Free parking is available on the premises.

Can I work from the cabin?

Yes. There is wifi and a dedicated workspace.

How many people can stay?

Up to four guests, in a single cabin with two bedrooms and two beds. You get the whole place to yourselves.

What are the check-in time, rates and cancellation terms?

These depend on your dates. Send an enquiry below and the hosts will confirm them. Bookings are completed on Airbnb.
